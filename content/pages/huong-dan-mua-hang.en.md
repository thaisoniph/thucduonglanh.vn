You can order from {brand} in any of the following ways:

## Why order directly on our website?

{uu_dai_web}

- **Genuine products directly from {brand}**, with no middlemen. Free shipping on eligible orders.
- **Inspect your goods before paying**, with easy returns and exchanges under our [return policy](/chinh-sach-doi-tra/).
- **A dedicated advisor** via Zalo/phone, plus reminders for using your products.

## 1. Order directly on the website (recommended)

1. Choose the product you want, select the quantity and click **Add to cart** or **Buy now**.
2. Go to the **Cart** to review your products and quantities.
3. Click **Proceed to checkout** and enter your full name, phone number and delivery address.
4. Choose a payment method and click **Place order**.
5. A {brand} staff member will call you to confirm your order during business hours.

## 2. Order via hotline / Zalo

Call or send a Zalo message to **{hotline}** with the product name, quantity and delivery address.

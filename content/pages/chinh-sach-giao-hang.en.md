## Delivery area

{brand} delivers nationwide through reputable shipping carriers.

## Delivery time

- Inner-city Hanoi: 1–2 business days.
- Other provinces and cities: 2–5 business days, depending on the area.

Delivery times may vary during public holidays, the Lunar New Year (Tet), or due to weather conditions or epidemics.

## Shipping fees

- Orders **under 300,000 VND**: shipping fee of **30,000 VND**, added to the order total.
- Orders of **300,000 VND or more**: **free shipping** nationwide.

The shipping fee is clearly shown at the ordering step before you confirm.

## Order tracking

Once your order has been shipped, we will provide a tracking number so you can follow your delivery. For any questions, please contact **{hotline}**.

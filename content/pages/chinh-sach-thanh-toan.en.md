{brand} accepts the following payment methods:

## 1. Cash on delivery (COD)

You pay the delivery person in cash after inspecting the goods.

## 2. Bank transfer

Transfer the payment to the bank account shown at checkout, entering your **order number** as the transfer description. Your order will be processed as soon as we receive the payment.

{brand} will never ask you for your password, OTP code or card details in any form.

## Listed prices

The price of each product is listed in Vietnamese dong (VND) on the product page and is shown again in the cart and at checkout before you confirm your order.

Prices listed on the website **include value-added tax (VAT)**.

## Shipping fees

Shipping fees apply according to our [Shipping Policy](/chinh-sach-giao-hang/) and are clearly shown at checkout:

- Orders under **300,000 VND**: shipping fee of **30,000 VND**.
- Orders of **300,000 VND** or more: free nationwide shipping.

## Price changes and promotions

When a promotion is running, the prices and conditions that apply will be announced directly on the website. The prices and promotions displayed at the time you confirm your order are the ones that apply to that order.

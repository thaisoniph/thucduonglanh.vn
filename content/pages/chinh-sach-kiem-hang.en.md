{brand} encourages you to inspect your goods upon delivery to protect your rights.

- You may **inspect the outside** of the products (quantity, type, packaging condition, expiry date) before paying the delivery person.
- Do not break the seal or open the product's lid before paying.
- If a product is dented, its packaging is torn, the product is wrong or the quantity is short, you have the right to refuse the delivery and should contact our hotline **{hotline}** right away for support.
- We recommend recording a video while unboxing, as evidence for the fastest possible resolution if any problem arises.

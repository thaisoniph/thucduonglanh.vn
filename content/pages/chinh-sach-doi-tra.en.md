## Cases eligible for return or exchange

- The product has a manufacturing defect or was damaged during shipping.
- You received the wrong product or the wrong quantity compared with your order.
- The product had already expired at the time of delivery.

## Conditions for return or exchange

- Notify {brand} within **7 days** of receiving your order.
- The product's seal must be intact (except in cases of a quality defect inside the product).
- Provide photos/videos showing the condition of the product.

## Costs

{brand} covers all shipping costs for the return or exchange if the fault is ours.

## Refunds

If, after verification, your order is eligible for a refund, {brand} will refund you by **bank transfer** within **7 business days**. Please provide accurate details of the receiving bank account so the refund can be processed smoothly.

## Cases not eligible for return or exchange

Products that have been used, products damaged by storage that does not follow the instructions, or returns/exchanges for personal reasons that do not fall under the cases above.

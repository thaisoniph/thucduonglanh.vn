{brand} always listens to and values all feedback from our customers.

## Step 1: Receipt

Send your feedback via hotline **{hotline}**, Zalo, email **{email}** or the contact form on our website, together with your order number and photos/videos (if any).

## Step 2: Verification

Our customer care team will get back to you within 24 business hours to verify the information.

## Step 3: Resolution

We will propose a solution (product exchange, refund, compensation, etc.) within 3–5 business days after verification is complete.

## Step 4: Response

We will notify you of the outcome. If no agreement can be reached, either party may request the competent authorities to resolve the matter in accordance with the law.

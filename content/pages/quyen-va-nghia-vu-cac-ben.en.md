## 2. Rights and obligations of the Company (website owner, seller)

**Rights**

- Refuse or cancel orders when customers provide fake or incorrect information, or when an order shows signs of fraud.
- Suspend or change website content for maintenance, upgrades, or at the request of state authorities.
- Ask customers to provide correct delivery information.

**Obligations**

- Publish truthful product information: name, ingredients, origin, price, expiry date, directions for use.
- List prices clearly, VAT included, shown again in the cart and at checkout.
- Sell only lawful goods of clear origin that meet legal requirements.
- Deliver on time and the right items; let customers inspect the outside of the package on delivery.
- Exchange, return and refund according to the published policy.
- Keep customers' personal information confidential; never sell, trade or rent it.
- Receive and handle feedback and complaints: verify within 24 working hours, propose a solution within 3–5 working days.
- Promptly remove information about goods that violate the law when requested by competent authorities.

## 3. Rights and obligations of the Buyer

**Rights**

- Receive complete, truthful information about products, prices, shipping fees and payment methods.
- Inspect goods on delivery.
- Exchange, return or get a refund under the return policy (defective, wrong or expired items).
- Have personal information protected.
- Send feedback or complaints and receive a response.

**Obligations**

- Provide correct and complete information when ordering: full name, phone number, delivery address.
- Pay the correct amount by the chosen method (cash on delivery or bank transfer).
- Not use the website for unlawful purposes, harm the website, or impersonate others.
- Not copy the website's images or content for commercial purposes without permission.

## 4. Disputes

- Both parties first try to resolve the matter together.
- Customers can send feedback via hotline {hotline}, Zalo, email {email} or the [contact form](/lien-he/) on the website.

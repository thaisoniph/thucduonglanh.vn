Modern life leads many people to skip breakfast, rush through lunch or snack without much thought. A healthy snack helps you keep your energy steady and avoid sudden hunger pangs.

## What makes a good snack?

- **Good carbs:** from oats, brown rice and whole grains – they release energy slowly.
- **Plant protein:** from beans and seeds – it keeps you full longer.
- **Healthy fats:** from macadamia nuts, walnuts, almonds and black sesame.
- **Fiber:** supports digestion.
- **Little refined sugar:** to avoid sudden spikes in blood sugar.

## A simple suggestion

If you don’t have much time to prepare, you can choose grain powders or ready-to-mix seed milks with clear ingredients, sweetened with stevia instead of table sugar. Just mix with warm water and you have a well-rounded snack.

---
title: 5 small habits for healthy living every day
excerpt: Healthy living doesn’t start with big changes, but with small, steady and sincere actions every day.
---

At Thực Dưỡng Lành, we believe healthy living is made of small actions repeated every day. Here are 5 simple habits our team practices.

## 1. Start your day with a glass of warm water

A glass of warm water after waking up is a gentle way to wake your body, instead of rushing to your phone or coffee.

## 2. Eat in harmony with nature

Choose foods close to their natural state: whole grains, beans and pulses, seasonal vegetables. Gradually cut back on processed foods and anything too sweet or too salty.

## 3. Write one line of gratitude each morning

Just one line: be grateful for a person, a meal, or a small thing that happened. Gratitude brings a calmer mind and a lighter view of life.

## 4. Slow down in a fast-paced life

Take a few minutes each day to eat slowly, breathe slowly, and enjoy a cup of tea without doing anything else. Every meal is a chance to come back to yourself.

## 5. Plant kindness starting today

You don’t need to wait for success before giving. A caring word, a vegetarian meal on the 1st and 15th of the lunar month, a small good deed – all of them are seeds of kindness.

*Nourish the Body – Calm the Mind – Lasting Health.*

Quang Tri is a land rich in history and blessed with many valuable medicinal plants. Among them, **chè vằng** (Jasminum subtriplinerve) and **đinh lăng** (Polyscias fruticosa) have long been part of local people’s daily life.

## What is a Geographical Indication?

A Geographical Indication is a sign protected by the State to identify products that come from a specific area, where the product’s quality or reputation is tied to that area’s natural conditions. **Quang Tri chè vằng** is protected by a Geographical Indication, managed by the Quang Tri Department of Science and Technology.

## The distinctive flavor of Quang Tri

Thanks to the region’s sun, wind and soil, Quang Tri small-leaf chè vằng has a gentle aroma, a mild bitterness and a naturally refreshing aftertaste. The raw material comes from cooperatives and farming households in Cam Lo, Hai Lang and Vinh Linh.

## From chè vằng leaves to a convenient cup of tea

Traditionally, chè vằng had to be dried, boiled and simmered for a long time. Gold Herbal Pharmaceutical Materials JSC uses **EECV** vacuum extraction combined with low-temperature drying at a GMP-certified factory to create [DILVANG Instant Herbal Tea](/san-pham/tra-hoa-tan-dilvang-dinh-lang-che-vang/) – just add warm water for a fragrant cup of herbal tea.

This deep processing also adds value to the medicinal plant, creating jobs and raising incomes for medicinal herb growers in Quang Tri.
